      Sources, scores and method
      Intelligence Index
      Artificial Analysis's combined score from about 20 independent evals (reasoning, science, coding, agent and knowledge tests), run by them on every model. 0 to 100; frontier models sit near 60.
      Arena score
      LMArena's Elo rating from blind human votes between two answers. It measures which answer people prefer, not whether it is correct.
      $ / 1M tokens
      Median hosted price, blended 3 parts input to 1 part output. Local models cost nothing per token.
      Fits your machine
      Ollama download size against your memory: "Runs great" at 40% of memory or less, "Runs" at 70% or less, leaving room for the system and your other apps. Checked on Apple Silicon Macs only; other machines show "Not checked".
      Uncensored build
      An abliterated copy published by huihui-ai on Hugging Face. Research shows these lose a little quality and lean overconfident.
      Refresh
      A timer on your machine runs a plain script each morning (no AI involved). It re-downloads every source and rebuilds this page. If one source fails, that section keeps its last good data and says so.
      Stars / day
      Stars gained per day over the last 7 days of daily snapshots. Until a week of snapshots exists, it is the lifetime average (stars divided by age), marked "avg".
      Rising on GitHub
      GitHub search for repos made in the last 60 days with 300+ stars, in your areas. Filtered out: no English description, bot-evasion, free-key resellers, crypto, games, trading.
      Watch outs
      Archived, no commits in 4+ months, unclear license, or copyleft license (GPL, AGPL: fine to use, careful about bundling into your own product).
      Findings history
      Every repo ever found stays in a ledger with the day it was first and last seen and its peak stars per day. Each morning the check writes findings: top 3 risers, new finds, breakouts (over 2x its recent pace and 50+ stars/day, needs 4+ days of data), releases, cooling (below 30% of its peak), repos that dropped off, and the hottest area.
      Stars per day by area
      Sum of stars gained between two daily snapshots, divided by the days between them, for every tracked repo in that area.
      Setup status
      "Have it" means installed on this machine. "Add next" comes from your watchlist's trial and to-do items first, then the fastest-rising repo in that area with no watch outs.
      Usage audit
      Reads your Claude Code session logs on this machine every morning (subagents included) and prices each reply at list rates from OpenRouter, cache writes and cache reads included. A model with no known price is shown as "unpriced" and never guessed. Only totals reach this page, never what you typed. Session titles and project names stay off unless you turn them on in ~/.model-desk/config.json.
      Job type
      Guessed from each session's title and first message (fix, plan, review, research, design and so on). A guess, so a few sessions land in the wrong job.
      Savings
      Estimates: the same tokens repriced at the suggested model, or the share of re-reading a change would remove. Quality is not measured here; the playbook keeps Opus for planning, debugging, review and design.
      Playbook source
      Anthropic's Claude API guidance: Sonnet for everyday coding and agent work, low effort for subagents and simple tasks, at least high effort for intelligence-sensitive work, and try the stronger model at lower effort before switching models.
      Not included
      LiveBench and Hugging Face's Open LLM Leaderboard (no machine-readable feed today), and speed measured on your own machine (the speeds shown are hosted).
